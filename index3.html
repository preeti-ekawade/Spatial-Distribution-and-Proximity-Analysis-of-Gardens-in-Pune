Data sources & methodology

  • Garden points: pune_gardens, classified by prabhag.
  • Polygons: garden_polygons, 100 m × 100 m envelopes built in EPSG:32643.
  • Pairs within 1 km: analysis_relationship_1km, from ST_DWithin at 1,000 m, classified by distance.
  • Nearest-garden links: analysis_nearest_garden.
  • Garden connectors: garden_connectors, six selected pairs.
  • Basemap: Esri World Light Gray Canvas, loaded online.

Distances are calculated in the browser from the coordinates (great-circle). CRS: EPSG:4326 for displayed layers.
